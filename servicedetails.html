Design System

A design system is a comprehensive set of standards, reusable components, and guidelines that helps teams build digital products at scale. It ensures consistency in design and user experience by providing a single source of truth for everything from brand visuals to interactive elements. By creating a shared language and a centralized library of assets, a design system enables designers and developers to work more efficiently and collaboratively. It ultimately helps a company maintain brand coherence and deliver a high-quality, unified experience to its users.

Digital Platforms

A digital platform is a business model that creates value by facilitating interactions between two or more interdependent groups, such as consumers and producers. These platforms leverage technology to connect participants and often benefit from network effects, where the platform becomes more valuable as more users join. They are built on data, allowing for personalized experiences and strategic growth. Ultimately, digital platforms act as a foundation for a vast ecosystem of users, developers, and services.
